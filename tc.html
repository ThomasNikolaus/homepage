Lecture course "Topological Cyclic homology", WS 2020/21

Topological Hochschild homology and Topological Cyclic homology are interesting invariants of rings with connections to stable homotopy theory, arithmetic geometry and algebraic K-theory. In this course,we want to give an introduction to this topic, based on the notes

Lectures.pdf (Warning: these contains some inaccuracies which will eventually be fixed)

Prerequisites are some familiarity with homotopy theory and homological algebra.
